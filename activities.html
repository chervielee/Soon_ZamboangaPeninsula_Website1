Activities & Experiences

Immerse yourself in coastal adventures, culinary tours, and heritage walks.

Adventure & Outdoor Experiences

  • Pink Beach Swimming & Snorkeling: Dive into clear waters surrounding Great Santa Cruz Island.
  • Jungle Trekking: Hike along scenic tropical paths leading directly to Merloquet Falls.
  • Vinta Regatta Sailing: Ride traditional native boats across the vibrant coastal waters of Region IX.

Cultural & Heritage Walks

  • Fort Pilar Historical Walk: Explore 17th-century stone ramparts and outdoor Marian shrines.
  • Dapitan Heritage Trail: Visit Dr. Jose Rizal's exile estate and outdoor amphitheater.
  • Yakan Weaving Experience: Watch indigenous artisans handcraft intricate colorful textiles.

Food & Culinary Tours

  • Curacha Seafood Feast: Taste deep-sea spanner crabs served in rich coconut Alavar sauce.
  • Native Satti & Coffee Tasting: Enjoy traditional spicy breakfast skewered meats with local coffee.
