Popular Destinations

Explore the natural wonders and landmark attractions across Region IX.

Great Santa Cruz Island

Famous worldwide for its rare pink sand beach composed of pulverized red organ pipe corals. Visitors can enjoy guided boat tours through pristine mangrove lagoons and experience protected marine eco-tourism.

Zamboanga City, Zamboanga del Sur

Fort Pilar Shrine

A royal 17th-century Spanish military defense fortress built to safeguard the region. Today, it stands as an open-air Marian shrine and houses a regional branch of the National Museum of the Philippines.

Zamboanga City

Rizal Shrine

The historical park marking the exact farm estate where Dr. Jose Rizal lived during his four years of exile from 1892 to 1896. The property features replicas of his bamboo houses, clinic, and outdoor amphitheater.

Dapitan City, Zamboanga del Norte

Merloquet Falls

A breathtaking multi-tiered waterfall hidden inside lush tropical woodland at Barangay Sibulao. The unique terraced rock formations allow cool freshwater to cascade gently like a curtain.
