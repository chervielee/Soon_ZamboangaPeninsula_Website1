Featured 3-Day Peninsula Itinerary

Experience the ultimate blend of heritage, pink sand beaches, and authentic gastronomy.

Day 1: Historic Arrival & Coastal Sunset

Zamboanga City
Morning Arrival & Cultural Warm-up

Land at Zamboanga International Airport. Check into your hotel and enjoy a warm local breakfast with hot Chavacano coffee.

Afternoon Fort Pilar Shrine & Museum Tour

Explore 17th-century Spanish stone ramparts, historic Marian shrines, and the National Museum branch exhibiting regional artifacts.

Evening Paseo del Mar Seafood Dinner

Stroll along the waterfront promenade and sample fresh seafood dishes as the sun sets over Basilan Strait.

Day 2: Pink Sand Island & Culinary Feast

Great Santa Cruz Island
Morning Vinta Boat Ride to Santa Cruz Island

Board a colorful traditional Vinta sailboat across clear turquoise waters to Great Santa Cruz Island, famous for its crushed red organ-pipe coral pink sand.

Afternoon Lagoon Snorkeling & Beach Relaxation

Swim, snorkel among stingless jellyfish in the protected lagoon, and relax under coconut palms on the pink shore.

Evening Authentic Curacha Feast

Dine at Alavar Seafood Restaurant for world-famous Curacha (spanner crab) generously covered in signature coconut cream Alavar sauce.

Day 3: Dapitan Heritage & Sunset Relaxation

Dapitan & Dipolog City
Morning Rizal Shrine Heritage Trail

Travel north to Dapitan City to visit Dr. Jose Rizal’s exile estate, outdoor amphitheater, and historical museum grounds.

Afternoon Yakan Weaving Village & Souvenir Shopping

Watch indigenous Yakan weavers craft intricate geometric textiles by hand and shop for handwoven bags and cloths.

Evening Dakak Beach Sunset View

Conclude your journey with a relaxing evening watching the sunset over Dakak Beach before airport departure.

Essential Travel Guide

Key tips and packing recommendations for a seamless trip across Region IX.

Travel Tips

  • Best visited during dry season months (November to May).
  • Chavacano, Tagalog, and English are widely spoken across Region IX.
  • Carry cash in Philippine Pesos (PHP) for local market and island purchases.
  • Book Santa Cruz Island boat slots early via the City Tourism Office.

Packing Checklist

  • Lightweight tropical clothes & extra swimwear
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, wide-brim hat & sunglasses
  • Waterproof dry bag for boat rides and island hopping
  • Comfortable walking shoes for heritage trail walks
